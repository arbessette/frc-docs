About FRC Documentation
=======================

This is a draft of robot-focused FRC documentation. The starter pages
establish the navigation and content boundaries for review. They are not a
complete robot-building manual or an official competition rule source.

WPILib remains the home for software and control-system procedures. This
site links to those procedures and adapts hardware reference articles
with attribution.
