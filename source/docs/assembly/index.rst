Assembly and Bring-up
=====================

Make the build easy to inspect, test, and repair. Keep drawings, wiring notes,
and changes with the robot's documentation instead of relying on memory.

Before assembly
---------------

See :doc:`../zero-to-robot/step-1/kitbot-starterbot-assembly` for official Kitbot
assembly resources, then check
:doc:`../yearly-overview/2027-overview` and use the wiring instructions for
your hardware.

* Confirm the parts and instructions match the planned design.
* Identify the tools, workspace, and mentor supervision needed for each task.
* Plan access to components that need adjustment, inspection, or replacement.

During assembly
---------------

* Record component locations and label cables and devices consistently.
* Review each completed subsystem before integrating it into the robot.
* Photograph areas that will be difficult to see after final assembly.

Bring-up record
---------------

Follow the applicable manufacturer and WPILib procedures for initial setup.
Record the test date, subsystem, expected behavior, observed behavior, and
remaining work. Resolve unexpected behavior before moving to the next stage.

Continue with :doc:`../competition/index` once your team has a repeatable
inspection, testing, and repair routine.
