Competition Preparation
=======================

Prepare the robot, tools, spare parts, and documentation before an event.

2027 season documents
---------------------

Use `FIRST's Game and Season page
<https://www.firstinspires.org/programs/frc/game-and-season>`_ and
`season materials
<https://www.firstinspires.org/resources/library/frc/season-materials>`_
for the current manual, team updates, and inspection checklist.
The 2027 game releases at Kickoff on January 9, 2027. Until the season
materials are published, don't use the previous season's robot limits or
inspection checklist as 2027 requirements.

Robot checklist
---------------

* Inspect mounting, wiring, strain relief, and battery connections.
* Bring the robot's wiring notes, device IDs, software versions, and test record.
* Pack the tools and spare parts needed to repair the components on your robot.
* Test the drivetrain and mechanisms before leaving for the event.
* When the 2027 checklist is available, complete a self-inspection and record
  anything that needs to be corrected.

For the controller update, see :doc:`../yearly-overview/2027-overview`.
